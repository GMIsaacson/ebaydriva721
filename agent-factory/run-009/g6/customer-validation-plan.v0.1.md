# Run 009 — G6 Controlled Customer Validation Plan v0.1

## Objective
Validate whether Twin Cities commercial electrical contractors find the municipal-development intelligence product valuable enough to use and potentially pay for, without expanding authority prematurely.

## G6 design authority
DESIGN_ONLY_NO_CONTACT.

Until explicit owner approval, G6 may:
- prepare a sample intelligence brief
- identify ideal customer profiles and public business candidates
- prepare draft outreach copy internally
- define pricing and validation metrics
- score responses if owner later authorizes outreach

G6 may NOT:
- send email, SMS, LinkedIn, contact forms, calls, or other outreach
- purchase lead lists, ads, domains, software, or data
- create paid subscriptions or invoices
- promise exclusivity or guaranteed project wins
- submit bids or contact developers/municipalities on a customer's behalf
- activate recurring customer delivery

## Ideal customer profile
Primary ICP: Twin Cities commercial electrical contractor that:
- serves commercial/multifamily/light-industrial construction
- has roughly 5–100 field employees or equivalent project capacity
- actively pursues new construction or major redevelopment work
- has a public website and business contact channel
- is not exclusively residential/service-call focused

Preferred buyer roles: owner, president, estimator, preconstruction lead, business development lead, or operations leader.

## Validation product
Customer sample should contain 3–5 QA-passing opportunities, not a raw municipal feed.

Each opportunity should show:
1. project/development name
2. municipality + site/location
3. project type and scale
4. current stage/timing signal
5. developer/owner/architect/GC only when evidenced
6. why it may create commercial electrical scope
7. confidence score
8. authoritative evidence/source link
9. recommended next research/action

Sample header proposition:
"Early Twin Cities development signals translated into commercial electrical opportunities — so your estimating/business-development team spends less time searching city records."

## Pricing hypotheses to test
- Founding pilot: $149/month for one trade/territory feed
- Standard hypothesis: $349/month
- Higher-value tier later: $499–$699/month with broader territory, enrichment, alerts, or multi-user delivery

Do not discount automatically. Do not quote a price externally until outreach/pricing authority is explicitly approved.

## Validation cohort
Initial controlled cohort target: 10–15 qualified contractors.

This is a validation cohort, not a mass-outreach campaign.

## Success metrics
G6 customer validation is considered commercially promising if, from the first 10–15 qualified prospects, we obtain a combination such as:
- >=5 meaningful responses OR conversations-equivalent written exchanges
- >=3 respondents explicitly rate the intelligence as useful/relevant
- >=2 request another report, trial, demo, territory example, or ongoing feed
- >=1 explicit willingness-to-pay signal at >=$149/month

Stronger pass:
- >=2 paid pilots or equivalent written purchase commitments.

Failure/iterate signals:
- <20% meaningful engagement after a properly targeted cohort
- repeated feedback that projects are too late, too generic, or not actionable
- buyers already receive equivalent intelligence cheaply/easily
- inability to identify decision-maker/public contact channels consistently

## Learning questions
Every validation interaction should answer as many as possible:
- Is the project early enough to matter?
- Which project fields are actually valuable?
- How much time does current project discovery consume?
- What sources/tools do they already use?
- Do they want daily, weekly, or immediate alerts?
- One city, metro-wide, or territory-based coverage?
- Which project types should be excluded?
- Who owns this problem internally?
- Would they pay $149 / $349 / $499+ and why?

## Outreach authority gate
Before any first external message, owner must explicitly approve:
- channel(s)
- number of prospects
- exact or bounded message template
- pricing language allowed
- sender identity/account
- whether follow-ups are allowed and maximum count

Default before approval: DRAFT_ONLY.

## Initial G6 sequence
1. Generate customer-ready 3–5 opportunity sample from validated data.
2. Build a 10–15 company ICP candidate list from public business information.
3. QA sample and ICP list.
4. Draft concise first-contact message internally.
5. Present validation pack to owner for explicit outreach authorization.
6. Only after approval, execute bounded outreach and measure results.

## Gate advancement
G6 DESIGN PASS means the experiment, authority, offer, metrics, and failure conditions are defined. It does not authorize outreach.
G6 LIVE VALIDATION PASS requires real customer evidence under separately approved external-action authority.
