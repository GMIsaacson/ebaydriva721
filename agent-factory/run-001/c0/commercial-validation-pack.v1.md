## Same-day pre-send gate
Before any owner approval can become executable, all four primary records must pass:
1. exact current Amazon ASIN/listing reopened;
2. brand/seller relationship checked;
3. current price, featured offer/Buy Box if visible, rating/review signal recorded;
4. current seller-created image/video/A+ state checked directly where observable;
5. weakness restated from current evidence only;
6. official public business contact route confirmed;
7. no opt-out or prior-contact conflict;
8. exact message fingerprint generated;
9. sender identity/email filled;
10. owner approves named recipients and exact final messages.

Any failure => HOLD or REPLACE. Never infer absence of video/A+ from incomplete snapshots.

## Bounded cohort
- Maximum first cohort: **4 sends**
- One message per company
- No automatic follow-up
- No phone calls
- No checkout link in first contact unless separately approved
- No promises of conversion, ranking, revenue, or sales lift

## Measurement plan
Track per recipient:
- delivered/submitted successfully;
- meaningful response;
- positive/useful response;
- request for sample/details;
- willingness-to-pay signal;
- paid pilot;
- objection/reason;
- operator minutes;
- evidence corrections / false-positive findings.

### C0 pass thresholds
**Minimum signal:**
- >=2 meaningful responses from 4 exposures, and
- >=1 prospect states the audit is useful/relevant or asks for more detail/sample.

**Commercial pass:**
- >=1 explicit willingness-to-pay signal at $299 **or** one paid pilot.

**Strong pass:**
- >=1 paid $299 pilot within the first 4-8 qualified exposures.

**Fail / redesign trigger:**
- 0 meaningful responses after 8 verified exposures, or
- responses consistently reject the problem/offer rather than timing/channel, or
- >25% of pre-contact audit hypotheses prove materially false during same-day verification.
