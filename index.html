Logistics and movement

Illustrative question

Where does a movement plan lose time, visibility, or accountability?

Likely constraint or dependency

Evidence boundary

Trust the distinction between known and modeled.

What a record can say

  • VERIFIED PUBLIC INFORMATIONInformation available from a public, inspectable source.
  • WORKING HYPOTHESISA proposition held open for testing.
  • ILLUSTRATIVE QUESTIONA prompt for inquiry, not a reported finding.

What this page does not claim

  • MODEL OUTPUTA possible artifact, not completed Meridian activity.
  • NOT A COMPLETED ENGAGEMENTNo example here represents a completed project, deal, relationship, or outcome.
