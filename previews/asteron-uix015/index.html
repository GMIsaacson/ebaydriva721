Method / evidence transitions

Every stage has a question to answer.

The route is not a promise of a result. It is a disciplined way to make progress, uncertainty, and stopping points visible.

Observe / what is present?

Review the available signal without treating it as a finding. Identify the source and what remains unknown.

Define / what is in scope?

Agree the population, period, rule, and evidence boundary before analysis proceeds.

Substantiate / what holds?

Test the defined issue against evidence and record whether it is substantiated, qualified, or stopped.

Authorize / who decides?

Identify the accountable decision owner. Authorization is an operating decision, not a certification.

Close / what confirms it?

Close against an agreed condition and retain a clear record of action, pause, or unresolved qualification.
