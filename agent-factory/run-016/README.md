## Coverage

The taxonomy has 20 named technology domains plus `emerging-unclassified`. Every run must report blind spots and silent sources. The team is prohibited from claiming that a finite source list proves nothing was missed.

## Output

The daily brief contract includes urgent changes, top developments, who built what where, prices and availability, medicine and life sciences, defense and battlefield technology, domain coverage, watchlist, rejected-noise summary, source health, and direct evidence links.

Urgent alerts can qualify in two ways:

- verified fact: strong evidence, score at least 82, confidence at least 75, and age no more than 48 hours;
- direct firsthand statement: primary attributable source, score at least 88, confidence at least 70, explicitly labeled as attribution rather than external verification.
